Motivation

Alternative data is a rich source of information that can be used to make investment decisions. It can provide insights that are not available in the market data and any type of factors or indicators. The faster you get the information, the better you can make the investment decisions. The Covid vaccine, Brexit, tarrifs, etc. are some examples of the events that moved the market and had a significant impact on the price of the stocks.

In 2024, I started a project to programatically read the news online, clean the text, and then find the sentiment of the news with the hope of using it for trading cryptocurrencies.

The data sources are the publicly available news websites which I will list in the data collection section. The sentiment analysis is done as a combination of classical and modern NLP techniques with a touch of proprietary techniques.

The project is ongoing and the results will be updated here.

Challenges and Lessons Learned
Challenge 1: Being detected by the websites as a bot

The first challenge was being detected by the websites as a bot. One of the most simple solutions is to rotate the headers of the headless browsers frequently.

Challenge 2: The headless browser and selenium left infant processes

This cause the EC2 or the server to run out of memory and crash. The hack for this is to restart the code every day to prevent the memory from being used up.

Challenge 3: How to get the sentiment of the news

The sentiment of the news is a very important part of the project. The challenge is to identify which coins are mentioned in the news. Then, what's the sentiment of the news for each coin. Also, there could be several aspects of a news that can be positive or negative for a coin. For example, security issues, market related news, etc.
